## Explore my work

### 🎯 1. AI value creation — Decisions to Value

**Where is the opportunity, what is it worth, and which decisions should improve?**

My approach starts with business performance and works backwards to identify decision and process losses, diagnose their causes, and prioritise interventions.

Topics I am documenting:

- **6Ds:** Drivers → Defect → Diagnosis → Drilldown → Doable → Dashboard
- **Decision Loss:** The value lost when a recurring decision underperforms the best feasible alternative
- **Five levels of Decision Intelligence:** From performance visibility to enterprise optimisation
- **AI opportunity assessment:** Connecting use cases to business cases and measurable outcomes

### 🔄 2. AI transformation

**What needs to change for AI to deliver value in day-to-day operations?**

- Decision architecture and operating model design
- Workflow redesign, human oversight and decision rights
- Governance, adoption and organisational change
- Benefits tracking and value realisation

### 🧩 3. AI product leadership

**What should we build, how should we build it, and how do we know it works?**

I am developing reusable product artefacts covering:

- Opportunity discovery, prioritisation and AI business cases
- Product strategy, requirements and architecture trade-offs
- Evaluation, guardrails and production readiness
- Product performance, adoption and continuous improvement

### 🤖 4. AI product prototypes

Practical demonstrations exploring how LLMs, agents, data, machine learning and optimisation can support better enterprise decisions.

**Planned / in development:**

- **Enterprise Value Intelligence:** Identifying and diagnosing value opportunities and recommending potential interventions
- **Agentic Marketing Optimisation:** Exploring AI-assisted marketing measurement and budget decisions

*Prototype documentation will distinguish concepts, working demonstrations and evaluated capabilities from production deployments.*
